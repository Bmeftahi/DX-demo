Inside the Inland Empire launch cluster

The Inland Empire spans dozens of separate permitting authorities — and because parcels compete across neighboring cities, developers actively city-shop. Jurisdiction intelligence concentrates here first, one city at a time. Illustrative sample notes below.

Riverside

The region's biggest city; active downtown infill multifamily pipeline.

Watch for: design review on infill sites; WQMP completeness comments.

San Bernardino

Value-oriented land basis; industrial and multifamily interest rising.

Watch for: liquefaction-zone geotech requirements; utility coordination timelines.

Redlands

Design-conscious review culture; strong retail and office-adjacent demand.

Watch for: stricter design standards; landscape and signage review depth.

Ontario

The logistics capital — high permit volume, experienced staff, fast cycles.

Watch for: truck circulation and queuing comments; warehouse ordinance buffers.
