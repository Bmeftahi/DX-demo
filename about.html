About Development Exchange

Commercial real estate development takes twenty-plus specialist disciplines. We're building the one place where they all get found, vetted, hired, and paid.

The problem we exist to fix

A developer starting a project today assembles their team through word of mouth, cold Google searches, and whoever answered the phone last time. SWPPP consultants, lighting engineers, geotechnical firms — each is discoverable only through their own website, if at all. There is no shared place to see who's good, who's licensed, who knows the local permitting office, and crucially, who has actually worked well together before.

Development Exchange puts the entire supply chain of a development project on one platform — matched by property type, jurisdiction, and verified track record.

How it works — for developers

1

Describe your project

Property type, jurisdiction, size. Build My Team proposes a full roster; Post a Project invites qualified firms to bid.

2

Compare & hire

Normalized bids with best-value scoring, side-by-side comparison, reviews, and mutually-confirmed collaboration history.

3

Run the project

A shared workspace for documents, timeline, messaging, and milestone payments held in escrow.

How it works — for specialist firms

1

List & verify

Create a profile for the firm and its licensed professionals. Licenses are verified against state boards; insurance documentation is kept on file.

2

Get matched

Appear in searches, team rosters, and RFP invitations that fit your specialty, property types, and jurisdictions — no cold outreach.

3

Build a track record

Every completed project adds reviews, confirmed collaborations, and code-fluency signal that compounds into future work.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a real, live product?

Not yet — this site is a working concept demo with fictional sample data. Every firm, review, message, and statistic is invented to demonstrate how the product would behave.

How do you verify firms?

Two distinct marks, deliberately not collapsed into one badge: “License verified” means the license number was checked against the state licensing board — a true verification. “Insurance documentation on file” means a certificate of insurance was collected directly and is tracked for expiration — documentation, not an independent validation of coverage. Reviews and collaboration history only count when tied to a project both parties confirm happened.

What stops firms from taking deals off-platform?

Nothing forcibly — and that's deliberate. The platform earns retention through the workspace, escrowed milestone payments, and the track record firms accumulate. Work done off-platform builds no reviews, no confirmed collaborations, and no code-fluency score.

How is the best-value bid score calculated?

Normalized price (40%), timeline (20%), and the firm's track record — on-time, on-budget, responsiveness, and code fluency (40%). Lowest price is often the wrong signal for a partner you'll work with for a year.

Which markets are covered?

One, deliberately: the Inland Empire — Riverside and San Bernardino counties — with recruiting concentrated in Riverside, San Bernardino, Redlands, and Ontario. Depth in a single market beats thin coverage across many, because a marketplace is only useful where it has enough firms to actually choose from. Los Angeles is the planned second market, followed by other high-volume metros.
