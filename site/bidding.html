Bidding / RFP

A sample project soliciting General Contractor bids. Compare bids on a normalized basis and see a best-value score, not just lowest price.

General Contractor Firm score Price Timeline Best-value score Notes
Best-value score blends normalized price (40%), timeline (20%), and the firm's overall track record — on-time, on-budget, responsiveness, code fluency (40%) — rather than ranking on price alone, which is often the wrong signal for a team you'll be working with for a year or more.

Bidder Q&A

Questions from bidding firms are anonymized and answered publicly to all bidders — everyone prices from the same information, the way a well-run RFP should work.
