Why the Inland Empire first

Big coastal developers have in-house benches and decades-old consultant relationships. The Inland Empire runs differently: small and mid-size developers — and plenty of first-time sponsors — assemble a team project by project. Add permitting that changes city by city across dozens of municipalities, and this is exactly the market a matching platform serves best.
